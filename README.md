# pathfinder-401

Authors: Abby Moll, Alex Severinghaus, Amisha Nambiar, Linda Diaz, Sania Rashid, and Sascha Manalili

This project is an AI-assisted faculty research intelligence platform that transforms fragmented academic information into trusted, searchable research profiles. It enables users to quickly identify faculty experts for research collaborations, funding opportunities, and other strategic needs.

The platform consolidates information from faculty CVs, university websites, scholarly publications, institutional award reports, research pages, and patent records. Because these sources may be incomplete or inconsistent, the system resolves records belonging to the same faculty member, reconciles discrepancies, preserves meaningful conflicts, and connects key claims to their original sources.

The completed platform helps USC Viterbi better understand its faculty’s research expertise, find relevant researchers more efficiently, and connect their work with external partnership and funding opportunities.
